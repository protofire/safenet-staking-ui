## What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your computer or mobile device when you visit. They enable the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time. Similar technologies include browser localStorage and pixel tags.

Cookies can be classified by lifespan — **session cookies** are erased when you close the browser; **persistent cookies** remain on your device for a pre-defined period.  Cookies can also be classified by domain: **first-party cookies** are set by the site you visit; **third-party cookies** are set by a different domain.

## Storage technologies and third-party services 

- **Strictly necessary / functional (localStorage):** the Interface stores non-personal preferences locally in your browser — such as interface theme, dismissed notices, and your last-used wallet connector — so you don't have to reconfigure them on every visit. This data never leaves your device.  
- **Wallet connection (third-party):** connecting a wallet via services such as WalletConnect may cause those services to set identifiers or establish sessions governed by their own policies. These are required for the wallet connection feature to work.  
- **Monitoring (third-party):** uptime and error monitoring tools (e.g. UptimeRobot, Sentry) may process technical request data to help us meet our availability and incident-response commitments.

We do not use cookies for advertising, targeting, or cross-site tracking, and we do not sell any data collected via cookies.

## Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings, and clear localStorage at any time. If you disable cookies or clear storage, the Interface will continue to work, but your preferences will not be remembered and some features (such as wallet reconnection) may require extra steps.
