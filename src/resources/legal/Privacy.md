# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Altoros LLC (dba Protofire DAO) ("Protofire", "we", "us", or "our") approaches privacy  in connection with your use of this Safenet Aegis staking web interface (the "Interface"). This Interface is designed to operate without collecting personal data.

By accessing or using the Interface, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

## 1\. No Personal Information Collected

Protofire does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information ("PII") such as your name, email address, phone number,  government identification, or other similar personal details, in connection with your use of the Interface.

The Interface does not require you to register, log in, or disclose any personal details. Your use of the Interface does not require you to provide any personal information to Protofire.

## 2\. On-Chain and Public Data

The Interface may display or process data that is publicly available on blockchain networks, such as: wallet addresses; SAFE token balances; staked amounts and validator delegations; pending and completed withdrawals; transaction history; and reward amounts and claim status.

This data is public by nature of the blockchain. It is not collected or stored by Protofire, but may be temporarily processed by the Interface (in your browser) to provide functionality such as displaying balances, withdrawal countdowns, validator information, and claimable rewards. Please note that blockchain data is permanent and public: your wallet address and transactions (including staking activity) are permanently visible to anyone, and Protofire has no ability to erase or modify them.

## 3\. Rewards Data

To display and enable reward claims, the Interface fetches publicly hosted rewards files (including Merkle proofs keyed by wallet address) published by the Safe ecosystem or other third parties. When your browser requests these files, your wallet address is transmitted directly to the server hosting them.  Protofire does not control such third-party hosting; their data handling is governed by their own policies.

## 4\. Local Storage and Technical Data

The Interface may store non-personal preferences (such as theme, dismissed notices, or last-connected wallet type) locally in your browser (localStorage). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared through your browser settings.

Protofire does not operate any server-side logging or monitoring infrastructure that captures technical data about your browser or device in connection with your use of the Interface. 

## 5\. Third-Party Services

The Interface interacts with third-party infrastructure providers, including but not limited to: RPC node providers; hosting providers (including IPFS gateways); rewards and validator data repositories; wallet connection services (e.g. WalletConnect); and uptime monitoring services (UptimeRobot \- uptimerobot.com/privacy). 

These third parties may collect data (such as your IP address or wallet address) in accordance with their own privacy policies. Protofire does not control how these third parties collect, use, or disclose data. By using the Interface, you acknowledge that your interaction with such services is governed by their respective terms and policies.

## 6\. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Interface's use of cookies and similar technologies is described in our [Cookie Policy](/cookies). You may adjust your browser settings to disable or manage cookies according to your preferences.

## 7\. Non-Sharing of Data 

Protofire does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any user data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes, as we do not collect any such data.

## 8\. Legal Requests

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or valid legal process, or where necessary to protect our rights, comply with sanctions obligations, or ensure the security of the Interface. As noted above, Protofire does not collect or store personal data, so any such disclosure would be limited to technical or operational information only. 

## 9\. Data Security

While Protofire does not collect user data, we implement reasonable security measures to ensure the Interface operates in a secure and reliable environment. However, we cannot guarantee the security of third-party services or the integrity of the blockchain networks you interact with. You are responsible for safeguarding your wallet credentials and devices.

## 10\. Children's Privacy

The Interface is not directed to children under the age of 18\. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a minor has provided information through the Interface, please contact us.

## 11\. Your Rights

Since this Interface does not process PII in a way that allows identification of individual visitors, data subject rights under applicable data protection law do not apply. In particular, certain rights such as the right to rectification or erasure cannot be enforced in relation to on-chain data, which is public, permanent, and immutable by nature. We do not create user profiles and we do not link blockchain addresses to identifiable individuals.

Where third parties process data in connection with your use of the Interface, we will reasonably assist in directing your request to the relevant third party.

## 12\. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" at the top of this page. Continued use of the Interface constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
